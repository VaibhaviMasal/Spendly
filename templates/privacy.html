Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly (the "Service") collects, how we use it, where it is stored, and the choices you have. It applies to your use of the Service and should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — the name and email address you provide when you register, and your password stored in hashed form. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Expense data — the records you enter into the Service, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions or notes you add.
  • Technical information — your IP address, browser type and version, device type, and the pages you visit within the Service.
  • Usage information — how you interact with the Service, such as the features you use and when you last signed in.

We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive financial identifiers such as your bank account numbers, card numbers, or net banking credentials. Please do not enter them anywhere in the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
  • Store your expense records and display them back to you, including totals, category breakdowns, and date-range summaries;
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service, including diagnosing faults and understanding which features are useful;
  • Communicate with you about your account, security matters, and changes to this policy or our Terms;
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising. We process your information on the basis that it is necessary to provide the Service you have asked for, and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure and working properly.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use a small number of essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your session from cross-site request forgery. These cookies are required for the Service to function; if you block them, you will not be able to stay signed in.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We do not sell or share cookie data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage

Your information is stored on servers operated by our hosting provider. We protect it using reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit between your browser and the Service, hashed password storage, and access controls that limit who can reach production data.

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your records or ask us to close your account, we will remove your personal information within a reasonable period. Some information may be retained for longer where we are required to do so by law, or where it is needed to resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or prevent fraud. Residual copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited time before being overwritten.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please choose a strong, unique password and keep your credentials to yourself.

5. Third Party Services

We rely on a limited number of third parties to run the Service:

  • Hosting and database providers — they store your account and expense data on our behalf, under contract and only on our instructions.
  • Font delivery — the Service loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, Google receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's own privacy policy for how it handles that data.
  • Email delivery — if we send you account or security emails, we use a third-party email provider, which processes your email address for that purpose only.

We do not share your expense records with these providers beyond what is needed to operate the Service. We may also disclose information where required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of the Service or its users. If the Service is ever transferred to a new operator, your information would transfer with it, and we would notify you beforehand.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can, at any time:

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate account details or expense records;
  • Delete individual expense records through the Service;
  • Request a copy of your expense data in a portable format;
  • Request that we close your account and delete your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable period and may need to verify your identity first. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of majority in their jurisdiction. If we learn that we have collected information from a child without appropriate consent, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Service, our practices, or applicable law. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Where a change is material, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you, for example by email or by an in-app notice, before it takes effect.

Your continued use of the Service after any change takes effect means you accept the revised policy. If you do not agree with it, you should stop using the Service and may ask us to delete your information.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or want to exercise any of the rights described above, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
